─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. KEY DISTINCTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Duplicate-token heads (Wang et al. 2022):
  - Identified only within IOI circuit for name repetition
  - 15 heads with elevated S2→S1 attention
  - Many are name/context-specific (low generalization)

Bloom filter heads (this paper):
  - 4 heads selected via capacity curves matching Bloom filter theory
  - 3/4 overlap with dup-token heads, but 1 (L3H0) is unique
  - Generalize broadly to ANY repeated token type
  - Show independence between heads (hash function property)
  - Exhibit false-positive rates matching theoretical predictions

VERDICT: Bloom filter heads are a SUBSET refinement — they identify
which duplicate-token heads implement a general-purpose, theoretically
principled membership-testing mechanism, vs. those that are
task-specific or context-dependent.
